AI NIDS — STEP 7: BENIGN-TRAINED ANOMALY DETECTOR

This package creates:

    src\07_anomaly_novelty_evaluation.py

WHY THIS STEP EXISTS

The supervised models are very strong for known attack patterns:

    Binary detector
        BENIGN versus ATTACK

    Attack-family classifier
        Denial of Service
        Port Scan
        Brute Force
        Web Attack
        Other Malicious

The complete scenario-holdout result showed that a supervised model struggles
when an attack family is absent from training. Step 7 adds a benign-trained
Isolation Forest that asks a different question:

    Does this flow look unusual compared with learned normal behavior?

An anomaly flag is not proof of an intrusion. It is a triage signal.

METHODOLOGY

For each of eight folds:

1. Exclude one complete traffic scenario.
2. Use BENIGN samples from the other seven only.
3. Fit SimpleImputer + IsolationForest.
4. Calibrate the threshold on BENIGN validation data only.
5. Target a 1% validation false-positive rate by default.
6. Evaluate every valid flow in the completely held-out scenario.

The script also trains a final benign-only artifact across all scenarios for
the later Streamlit SOC dashboard.

INTERPRETATION

A strong anomaly result would mean unseen attacks often differ from learned
benign behavior. A high held-out benign false-positive rate would reveal normal
traffic distribution shift. Both outcomes are valuable and must be reported
honestly.
